Safety acknowledgement

The rules we follow when working with the arm, grouped by when each one applies.

Before we operate

Risk assessment

Ensure safety measures are established exactly as defined in the technical risk assessment.

Workspace setup

Operate the arm in a clear, unobstructed area within your direct line of sight.

While the arm is running

Emergency protocols

Perform an emergency stop immediately if the host software displays an error message.

Payload limits

Ensure the effective load never exceeds the maximum 3KG capacity during use.

Physical proximity

Ensure individuals keep their heads, faces, and other body parts outside the arm's reach before starting and during operation.

Looking after the hardware

Hardware integrity

Do not modify the robotic arm, as modifications can create unpredictable hazards for integrators.

Environmental limits

Do not expose the robotic arm to permanent magnetic fields, which can damage the system.
